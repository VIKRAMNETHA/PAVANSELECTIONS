About Us

A Family-Run Legacy Since 2010

Pavan Selections has been a part of Korutla's heart since 2010 — a family-run store built on honesty, quality and personal attention. We believe great menswear starts with great fabric, which is why we hand-pick every bolt of suiting and shirting material that reaches our shelves.

From everyday shirting to wedding-ready suiting, from readymade formals to expert custom tailoring — we offer quality fabrics at fair prices, with the kind of service only a family business can give.
